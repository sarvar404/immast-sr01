Over 14000 surgeons & physicians have been trained so far at IMMAST since inception with outstanding feedback from all of the participants: IMMAST received the participants from more than 30 countries.

IMMAST offers the promise of not just providing structured learning through unique hands on practice sessions, but also supports its International and National alumni with ongoing advice from faculty which includes the who's who of medicine and surgery.

The Course Design & Delivery at IMMAST ensure a scientific & comprehensive curriculum, an optimal mix of virtual reality simulation, didactics, live procedure observation, hands-on practice and group discussions, creating an unmatched learning experience. IMMAST is one of the handful of centres in the world to focus on procedure, and not product-based training. At IMMAST, courses are designed to teach the complete healthcare team the physician, the surgeon, the nurses, paramedical and support staff. The aim is to create safe healthcare settings - clinical and surgical, with confident teams that are well versed in standard procedures.

Sehgal Neo Hospital, formerly known as Sehgal Nursing Home located in west Delhi, was started in the year 1991 with an objective of strengthening medical care for all sections of society by providing superior health care at an affordable cost. The unmatched quality of care, and the excellent clinical outcomes led to an ever increasing demand for out and in-patient treatment from not only the immediate catchment but also from far flung areas. This huge unmet need inspired the team to expand and upgrade it to what is now one of the top hospitals in this part of town. It is now a multi-specialty, state of the art, 100 bed hospital with the highest level of technology and service levels.
 
The man behind this vision and success is its Medical Director Dr. Narin Sehgal an MBBS (General Surgery), FIAGES, DGE (Germany), Senior Consultant and Head of Department of Minimal Access, General and Bariatric Surgery.

SNH boasts of four modular operation theaters, highly efficient neonatal and adult ICU, Luxurious labour delivery rooms, sophisticated emergency & trauma support center, dialysis centre and a Dental center. It also has a 24 hours, state of the art Laboratory services managed by “Dr. Lal Path Labs Pvt. Ltd and an imaging center.  
Over the years SNH has touched 300000+ lives- from offering routine wellness & preventive health care to innovative life-saving surgical treatments OPD, Surgeries, IPD.
